# Builders

Build a peptide, a DNA or RNA strand, or a metal complex, then open it as a new document or add it to the molecule you are editing.

## Open a builder

The **Compute** menu in the bottom corner of the 3D view lists the builders for a single-file scene, including plain MOL and SDF files. They are also in the command palette.

| Builder | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| **Build peptide…** | Sequence in one-letter or three-letter codes, L or D residues, backbone conformation (φ and ψ), N- and C-terminus, and chain. |
| **Build DNA or RNA…** | Sequence, nucleic acid type, helix form, and strands. |
| **Build metal complex…** | Metal, oxidation state, coordination geometry, and the ligand at each site, with the bond length for one- and two-site ligands. The total charge is shown as you edit. |

**Preview** shows the result in the dialog. **Create** opens it as a new document.

## Insert into an open file

When the active document is an editable MOL or SDF file, the peptide, DNA or RNA, and metal-complex builders show **Insert** next to **Preview**.

1. Open the MOL or SDF file and choose a builder from **Compute**.
2. Set up the structure and click **Insert**. **Inserted** confirms the change.
3. The new molecule is placed beside the existing structure, and the view widens to show both.

![The Build metal complex dialog over a caffeine SDF: Pt(II), square planar, two chlorido and two ammine ligands, with Inserted next to the Insert button.](/assets/docs/avogadro/builder-metal-complex.webp)

Each insertion is one Undo step in the document's edit history, so **Undo** removes it and **Redo** brings it back. Use **Save copy** to write the combined file without replacing the original. Editing is limited to 256 atoms in total, including the inserted molecule.

PDB and mmCIF files do not receive inserted residues. For those, the builders open the result as a new document that you can combine with the original in a scene. **Build polymer…** has no Insert because it works on the open document itself.

## Metal complexes

The ligand list holds 76 ligands in six groups: **Monodentate**, **Haptic**, **Bidentate**, **Tridentate**, **Tetradentate**, and **Hexadentate**. Among them are cyclopentadienyl, benzene and other η-rings, bipyridine and phenanthroline, terpyridine, tacn and Tp, porphyrin, salen and cyclam, edta, and 18-crown-6.

Coordination geometries run from linear (2 sites) to square antiprismatic (8 sites), including trigonal prismatic, pentagonal bipyramidal, and hexagonal bipyramidal.

- A ligand that binds through several atoms takes as many sites. Pick it for one site, and Burette fills the others it fits.
- **Other sites** next to the ligand lists every set of sites the ligand can reach, so you can choose between arrangements such as *mer* and *fac*, or cis positions. It is hidden when a ligand fits the geometry only one way, as edta does in an octahedron.
- Ligands that cannot reach a site from the current choice are disabled in the list.
- Chelates of three or more donors keep their own shape and bite angles. For one- and two-site ligands you can type the metal–donor distance; for a haptic ring it is the distance from the metal to the ring centre.

![Ru(II) with two terpyridine ligands in an octahedron. Each terpyridine fills three meridional sites, and the Other sites lists show With 2, 3 and With 5, 6. The total charge is +2.](/assets/docs/avogadro/builder-metal-terpy.webp)

![Ferrocene built from Fe(II), a linear geometry, and two cyclopentadienyl rings at 1.67 Å from the metal to each ring centre. The total charge is 0.](/assets/docs/avogadro/builder-metal-ferrocene.webp)

If a ligand does not fit the chosen sites, the message names the sites and asks for other sites or another geometry.

## Dative bonds

Metal–donor bonds of a built complex are inserted as dative bonds. The **Edit** panel lists them with the order **Dative**, and offers **Dative** as a bond order when you select a bond to a metal. They are saved as dative bonds in the MOL or SDF file.

![The Edit panel after inserting cisplatin into caffeine: the N52–Pt45 bond is selected with the order set to Dative, and the bond list shows the dative metal–nitrogen bonds.](/assets/docs/avogadro/builder-dative-bonds.webp)
